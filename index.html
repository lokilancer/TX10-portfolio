01WHO WE ARE

A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

A team that likes
to figure things out.

TMMV Robotics brings together students who are interested in discovering how technology works — and what we can create with it.

From robotics and programming to hands-on experiments, we believe the best way to learn is to ask questions, try ideas and improve through teamwork.

This website is our shared space to introduce our people, projects and experiences.
